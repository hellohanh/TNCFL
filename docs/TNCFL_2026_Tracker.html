Playoff Rules & Payouts
Format
• Opt-in — $50/manager. Open to all 10 managers; ask the league after Week 11.
• Seeding — ESPN standings at end of Week 13 (9–10 participants) or Week 14 (4–8 participants). If a manager opts out, seeds shift down to fill the gap.
• Scoring — Weekly fantasy score (not head-to-head record). Highest score in each matchup advances.
• Ties — Resolved by total cumulative points.
