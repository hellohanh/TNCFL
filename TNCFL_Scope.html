Thursday Night Curse Record Book

Product scope · living doc · last updated 2026-10-17 (Ledger Entry 116)

What it is

The TNCFL Record Book is the all-time archive website of the Thursday Night Curse Fantasy League, a points-based fantasy football league running since 2011. It tells each season's story — who won, who got cursed, where the money went — and keeps the league's permanent records, rivalries and Hall of Fame in one place. It is live at hellohanh.github.io/TNCFL and installs on a phone like an app.

It replaced the old single-file "TNCFL Hub" with a modern, scroll-driven design: same data, same numbers, rebuilt to be more immersive and interactive, season by season from 2011 forward. The live 2026 season is tracked separately by the 2026 Tracker; the Record Book covers completed seasons only.

The non-negotiable rule: every number on the site — scores, tallies, droughts, records, payouts — is derived from the verified HMOTW engine's data feed. Nothing is hand-typed. Only prose (recaps, nugget cards, the Dancing Shoes roast, manager bios) is written by hand.

Who it's for

  • League managers (18 all-time, ~10 active) looking up a season, a rivalry or their own career.
  • Hanh, the league's historian and the site's owner, who curates the story and verifies the numbers.
  • Mostly on phones (Galaxy S25 is the reference device) as well as desktop.

What's on it

Seasons — 15 chapters, 2011 to 2025

Every season uses the same locked template (proven on 2011, then rolled out to all 15):

The story

Headline, recap essay, the champion's total with a week-by-week sparkline, stat tiles, and a scroll-driven flea-flicker play animation on the latest season (desktop).

The scoreboard

Every manager's weekly score with high/low/200+ pills, sortable; a toggle to focus on the Money Circle; click any manager to pin them across every chart.

The race

Weekly Burnout — an F1-style race of the standings with a countdown and replay; Scoring Spread Index; the Despair Differential.

The curse

Drought bars ("Thirsting for a W"), the Dancing Shoes roast of the driest manager, the all-time Longest Drought list, Peak Performance pyramids.

The trivia

Nugget cards — hand-written fact finds for each season — plus the Cumulative Competence chart and all-time highest/lowest single weeks.

The money

Salary-cap rules, the full 16-column ledger, winners & losers donuts — for the season and for each manager's whole career.

How it feels on a phone

  • A yellow handle on the left edge opens the page menu (the 2026 Tracker's drawer, mirrored).
  • Wide charts and tables are drawn sideways — turn the phone to read them full size. Every sideways panel is the same height and fills the screen.
  • Long lists show the top 15 and scroll; side-by-side panels stack; nothing runs off the screen.
  • Sections build as you scroll near them, so tapping between pages stays quick.

Out of scope

  • The live 2026 season tracker, its rules document and its Cloudflare Worker (separate project, linked from the menu as "2026 ↗").
  • Data entry and the drought engine itself — the Record Book reads the engine's output, it doesn't calculate league results.
